WHAT IS THE WAREZ SCENE

The warez scene emerged in the early 1980s as personal computers became common and software publishers began adding copy protection to their products. Release groups competed to crack these protections and distribute software freely. Groups had names, internal hierarchies, and credited their members on each release. Crackers broke the protection. Couriers distributed the files across BBS networks overnight. This tracker documents releases logged by active crews.
